How Basketball Works


Basketball is a team sport played by two teams.

The main goal of basketball is to score points. Players score by shooting the ball into the opponent's basket. Players can dribble, pass, and shoot the ball while playing.

How You Score

Free Throw = 1 point

Regular Shot = 2 points

Three-Point Shot = 3 points

Basic Rules

Players must dribble the ball when moving around the court. Teams must also avoid fouls such as pushing or hitting other players.
